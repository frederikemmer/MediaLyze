# Roadmap

MediaLyze is evolving toward a clearer, faster way to understand and maintain large media collections. This roadmap highlights the next user-facing improvements; priorities and timing may change.

## Working On

- **Hardware-aware transcoding** — convert videos with validated CPU or hardware paths, clear progress, flexible output options, and linked comparisons.
- **Transcoding across trusted installations** — pair MediaLyze installations and use available workers for larger conversion jobs.
- **Better device and player compatibility profiles** — expand the catalog and make compatibility decisions easier to understand.

## Planned

- **Guided onboarding** — help new installations connect their media, choose sensible defaults, and complete the first scan.
- **Richer statistics and comparisons** — add more useful metrics, visualizations, and ways to compare libraries and files.
- **A more consistent experience** — continue aligning settings, file details, and everyday workflows across MediaLyze.
- **Notifications** — make important scan, maintenance, and transcoding events visible without requiring constant manual checking.

## Backlog

- **More interface languages** — make MediaLyze accessible to more users around the world.
- **More media-server integrations** — extend the existing connector system with Plex and selected services from the Arr stack.
